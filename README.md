# Book-Pulse
BookPulse is an end-to-end reading intelligence system. Enter the books you've read and your TBR pile, and it learns your taste with TF-IDF and cosine similarity, ranks what to read next, and explains why. A Serendipity Dial, Roll the Dice, and Blind Spot insights add surprise. No dataset needed, only your shelf. FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, React.
